# Chennai accident spots: where each one comes from

Added from public reports (searched on 14 November 2026). **Only places that a report actually names are included.**
The full lists are not published: Chennai police say they identified 104 black spots, and the state says 3,500, but the
names are not in the public reports. So this is a starting list, not a complete one.

The coordinates come from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) for the place name, so each is the middle of the named place, not a
surveyed point. Check them against the road before relying on them, and edit them in the admin panel if needed.

## Left out on purpose
* *Ponneri High Road - Andarkuppam junction* (same Ennore study), *Kattankulathur*, *Thailavaram* and *Perumbakkam-Mambakkam*
  (NHAI 2011-13 data): named in reports, but OpenStreetMap did not give a reliable point for them.
* *Bukkathurai* and the Salem and Villupuram stretches: too far from Chennai.
* Counts without names (104 Chennai black spots, 3,500 in Tamil Nadu, 1,208 in 2015-18): there is nothing to place on a map.

## The district zones
The 37 Tamil Nadu zones (TN01-TN37) are whole districts with a 5 km radius (for example "Chennai - 975 road deaths").
They are not accident spots. The map now draws a district only as a thin dashed outline while you are inside it, never as a
filled circle, and only the spots near the car are drawn.
